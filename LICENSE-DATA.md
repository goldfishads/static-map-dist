# Data attribution

- `dist/data/us-states.js`, `dist/data/us-counties.js`: US Census Bureau, cartographic boundary files 2023 (1:20,000,000). Public domain.
- `dist/data/us-towns.js`: US Census Bureau, 2020 urban areas, cartographic boundary file (1:500,000), reduced to one point per area. Public domain.
- `dist/data/us-interstates.js`, `dist/data/us-highways.js`: Natural Earth 1:10m roads. Public domain.
- `dist/data/world-countries.js`, `dist/data/world-lakes.js`: Natural Earth 1:50m countries and lakes. Public domain.
- `dist/data/world-regions.js`: Natural Earth 1:10m state and province lines. Public domain.
- `dist/data/world-cities.js`: Natural Earth 1:10m populated places. Public domain.

Pages that use the script show the attribution line "Boundaries, roads and places: Natural Earth, US Census Bureau".
